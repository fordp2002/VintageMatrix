## Context

The README describes the app at length but has no single sentence that says what it is and who it is for. That sentence anchors scope decisions and the Manifesto's "stay on topic" test.

## Known constraints

- Must cover both the app and the framework, and both human and AI users (PRIN-003).
- Must keep the vintage focus (00-overview scope).

## Options

None recorded yet.

## Decision

> This project is for anyone designing a matrix-based keyboard PCB and/or firmware to support a matrix keyboard. Its focus is on keyboards that are non-standard by today's standards.

Notes:

- **Audience:** anyone designing a matrix keyboard, not only people recreating vintage machines. Vintage home computers and serial terminals are the main examples of "non-standard by today's standards", not the boundary of the project.
- **"PCB and/or firmware":** a user may want only one of the two. This is now a principle (PRIN-008).
- Modern standard layouts remain possible but are not the focus.
